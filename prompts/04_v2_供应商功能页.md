# 申请表单（/leads/[id]/apply）

**页面标题：** "Apply to This Lead"
**副标题：** "Your application will be reviewed by the buyer. Be specific and highlight your strengths."

**表单（白色卡片，圆角12px，内边距32px）：**

**Why We're a Good Match（必填）**
标签："Why is your factory suitable for this order?"
文本区域（5行）：
占位文字："e.g. We specialize in fine gauge knitwear with 15 years of export experience. We have supplied similar products to brands in Europe and the US. Our MOQ is 300 pcs/style..."

**Specific Capability（针对此订单）**
标签："Can you meet the requirements?"
两列：
- 确认MOQ：[Yes, we can meet 500 pcs/style] / [Our MOQ is: ___pcs]
- 价格范围：[Within range] / [Our price: FOB USD ___]
- 交期：Lead time: ___ days for samples / ___ days for bulk

**参考样品/产品（可选）**
标签："Reference Products (Optional)"
上传区域（紧凑型）：JPG/PNG，最多5张，"Upload photos of similar products you've made"

**联系确认**
标签："Confirm Your Contact"
预填显示（只读）：WhatsApp: +65 xxxx xxxx
小字："Buyer will contact you via WhatsApp if they have questions"

**提交按钮：**
[Submit Application →] 深蓝主要按钮，全宽

提交后弹窗确认："Application submitted! We'll notify you when the buyer responds."
